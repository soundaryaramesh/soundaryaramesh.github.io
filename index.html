Hey there! I'm Soundarya.

Postdoctoral researcher at the Augmented Human Lab, NUS.

I'm currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Augmented Human Lab, in the National University of Singapore (NUS). I work on machine learning for sensing systems that enhance human safety and well-being, focusing on audio and physiological time-series data. Recently, I've also been exploring multimodal approaches for audio-centered applications such as language-queried enhancement.

Prior to my postdoc, I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at NUS, on the topic of audio security and privacy, under the supervision of Prof. Jun Han and Prof. Chan Mun Choon.
